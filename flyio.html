☁️ Concept: Fly.io

What is Fly.io?

Fly.io is a platform-as-a-service (PaaS) that transforms standard Docker/OCI containers into micro-VMs running on bare-metal servers positioned in edge data centers all over the world. It brings your application closer to your users to drastically reduce latency.

How Does It Work?

Fly.io intercepts standard HTTP and TCP traffic via their global Anycast network and routes it to the nearest region where your container (Fly Machine) is deployed. Under the hood, it uses Firecracker micro-VMs (the same technology powering AWS Lambda) to provide strong hardware virtualization isolation combined with sub-second boot times.

Why Pair it with Podman?

Because Fly.io builds and deploys standard Docker/OCI images targeting the linux/amd64 architecture, developers using Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) need a reliable way to emulate and test that architecture locally. Podman natively integrates with QEMU to run amd64 containers on Macs, ensuring parity between your local environment and Fly.io's production servers.
